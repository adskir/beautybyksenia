The Technique

What is Lip Blushing?

Lip blushing is a form of permanent makeup that deposits soft pigment into your lips to enhance their natural color, shape, and definition. The result looks like your lips — but better. Fuller, more symmetrical, with a natural flush of color.

Unlike lip fillers, lip blushing doesn't add volume — it enhances what you already have. It's the perfect solution if you want to even out discoloration, define your lip border, or simply wake up with beautiful lips without reaching for lipstick.

The Process

How It Works

01

Consultation

We discuss your goals, select the perfect shade together, and map the lip shape. No two lip designs are the same.

A strong topical numbing cream is applied to your lips for 30 minutes. Most clients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure feels.

FAQ

Lip Blushing Questions

What is lip blushing and how is it different from lip tattoo? +

Lip blushing is a modern, softer form of lip tattooing. Traditional lip tattoos used heavy ink and created very bold, opaque results. Lip blushing uses semi-permanent pigments applied with a lightweight technique to create a natural, sheer flush of color — like your lips on a good day. The result fades gradually and can be refreshed over time.

How long does lip blushing last? +

Lip blushing typically lasts 1 to 2 years. Factors like sun exposure, the products you use on your lips, and your skin's natural cell turnover affect longevity. A touch-up session at 12–18 months keeps the color looking fresh.

Does lip blushing hurt? +

We apply a strong numbing cream before the procedure, and re-apply during the session as needed. Most clients find the lips easier to numb than brows. Discomfort is usually minimal — many clients fall asleep during the procedure.

Can I get lip blushing if I've had fillers? +

Yes — but we recommend waiting at least 4 weeks after any filler injection before getting lip blushing. If you plan to get fillers after lip blushing, wait at least 2 weeks. We'll discuss your full treatment history during the consultation.

How much does lip blushing cost in NYC? +

Our lip blushing service is $300 this month (normally $600). This includes the full procedure, custom shade mixing, and a follow-up touch-up appointment at 4–6 weeks. No hidden costs.

What's the healing process like? +

Immediately after the procedure, lips will look darker and feel slightly swollen — this is normal. Over days 3–7 the color will peel and appear very light. By week 4–6 the true healed color settles in. We provide a full aftercare kit and instructions to ensure the best result.
