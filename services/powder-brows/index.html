The Technique

What are Powder Brows?

Powder brows (also called ombre brows or shading brows) are a form of permanent eyebrow makeup. Using a digital machine, tiny dots of pigment are deposited into the skin to create a soft, powdery effect — like a perfectly applied eyebrow powder.

Looking for microblading in Brooklyn? Many clients come to us searching for microblading near me and leave with powder brows instead — because they last longer, heal more evenly, and work on all skin types including oily skin.

FAQ

Powder Brows Questions

How is powder brows different from microblading? +

Microblading uses a hand tool to create hair-like strokes by cutting into the skin. Powder brows use a digital machine to deposit tiny dots of pigment, creating a soft powdery effect. Powder brows last longer (1–2 years vs 6–12 months), heal more evenly, and work on all skin types — especially oily skin where microblading tends to fade quickly.

How long do powder brows last? +

Powder brows typically last 1 to 2 years depending on your skin type, lifestyle, and sun exposure. Oily skin and frequent sun exposure may cause faster fading. A touch-up at 12–18 months keeps everything looking fresh.

Do powder brows hurt? +

Most clients are surprised at how comfortable the procedure is. We apply a professional numbing cream before the session, and the machine creates less trauma to the skin compared to microblading. Most people rate the discomfort at 2–3 out of 10.

How much do powder brows cost in Brooklyn? +

Our powder brows service is $300 this month (normally $600). This includes the full procedure and a follow-up touch-up consultation at 4–6 weeks. No hidden fees.

How do I prepare for powder brows? +

Avoid alcohol, blood thinners, and retinol products for 48 hours before your appointment. Don't work out the morning of your session. Come with your natural brows — no need to fill them in. Bring reference photos if you have a shape in mind. We'll map everything together during the consultation.

Can I get powder brows if I already have microblading? +

Yes, in most cases. If your existing microblading has faded sufficiently, powder brows can be applied over or around the strokes to refresh and improve the result. We'll assess your brows during a free consultation before booking.
