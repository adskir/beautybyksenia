The Technique

Why Go Permanent?

Permanent eyeliner — also called eyeliner tattoo or cosmetic eyeliner — uses a digital machine to deposit pigment along your lash line. The result is a defined, symmetrical line that doesn't smudge, doesn't fade by noon, and doesn't require a steady hand every morning.

Perfect for contact lens wearers, athletes, and anyone who's ever walked out of a pool looking like a raccoon. Your eyeliner stays put through workouts, rain, tears, and everything in between.

◈ Smudge-proof & waterproof — stays perfect through sweat, rain, and tears
◈ Saves time every morning — no more uneven wings or starting over
◈ Safe for sensitive eyes — no daily contact with eye makeup removers
◈ Lasts 1–2 years — gradual, natural fade with optional touch-up
◈ Perfect symmetry — both eyes matched precisely, every time
The Process

How It Works

01

Consultation

We discuss your eye shape, desired style, and thickness. I'll recommend what flatters your features most — then draw on the line before committing to pigment.

02

Numbing

A topical numbing cream is applied to the eyelid area. Most clients find the procedure surprisingly comfortable — far less intense than they expected.

03

Procedure

Pigment is placed precisely along the lash line using a fine digital needle. The session takes 1.5–2 hours depending on the chosen style.

04

Healing

Eyelids heal in 5–7 days. The line appears darker initially, then softens to its true color. A complimentary touch-up at 4–6 weeks perfects the result.

FAQ

Eyeliner Tattoo Questions

Is permanent eyeliner safe? +

Yes — when performed by a licensed, experienced artist using sterile, single-use needles and professional-grade pigments. Ksenia has 30 years of experience with permanent eye makeup and follows strict hygiene protocols at every session. We use pigments specifically formulated for the delicate eye area.

Does eyeliner tattoo hurt? +

Most clients are surprised by how tolerable it is. The eyelid area numbs very effectively, and many clients describe the sensation as a slight vibration rather than pain. Discomfort is typically rated 2–3 out of 10. The procedure takes 1.5–2 hours total including numbing time.

How long does permanent eyeliner last? +

Permanent eyeliner typically lasts 1 to 2 years. Factors like sun exposure, skin type, and the products you use around your eyes affect longevity. The line fades gradually and naturally — it never looks abrupt or patchy. A touch-up at 12–18 months keeps it fresh.

Can I wear contact lenses after the procedure? +

We recommend removing contact lenses before the procedure and not wearing them for 24–48 hours afterward while any initial swelling subsides. After that, you can resume wearing them normally.

How much does eyeliner tattoo cost in Brooklyn? +

What should I avoid before getting permanent eyeliner? +

Avoid blood thinners, alcohol, and caffeine for 24 hours before your appointment. Do not use retinol or exfoliating products around the eye area for one week prior. Remove lash extensions at least 1 week before (they need to be fully grown out). Come with clean eyes — no mascara or eye makeup on the day of your session.
