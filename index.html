FAQ

Common Questions

How long does permanent makeup last? +

Typically 1 to 2 years, depending on skin type, lifestyle, and aftercare. A touch-up session at 6–12 months keeps everything looking fresh and extends the life of your permanent makeup.

Is powder brows better than microblading? +

Powder brows last longer (1–2 years vs. 1 year for microblading), work on all skin types including oily skin, and heal more evenly. Many clients searching for microblading near me in Brooklyn choose powder brows after a consultation — the results are softer and more natural long-term.

Does it hurt? +

Most clients describe it as mild discomfort. I apply a professional numbing cream before every procedure to keep you comfortable throughout the session.
